Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR)

Modern justice systems increasingly encourage parties to resolve disputes through Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) mechanisms before resorting to litigation. ADR offers a faster, confidential, and more cost-effective way of resolving conflicts while preserving business and personal relationships.

At Tsapy & Partners, we assist clients throughout mediation, conciliation, and arbitration proceedings. Our ADR specialists ensure that clients’ interests are clearly expressed and legally protected in every type of settlement process.

Mediation & Conciliation

These methods are ideal for clients seeking amicable and time-efficient solutions. Mediation and conciliation are particularly suitable for:

  • Contract breaches
  • Divorce and family disputes
  • Labour disputes
  • Debt recovery

We support clients in designing settlement agreements, ensuring enforceability and the protection of their rights.

Arbitration

Arbitration is the preferred mechanism for many companies because it offers binding resolutions without going through lengthy court procedures. It is confidential, efficient, and highly adapted to commercial disputes.

Tsapy & Partners collaborates with mandated arbitrators, including external partners in Rwanda and Senegal, to deliver high-quality arbitration services and optimal outcomes for complex business disputes.
