Employment & Compensation

Introduction

Managing a modern workforce requires careful compliance with labour regulations, strategic planning, and effective human resource management. At Tsapy & Partners, we assist employers across multiple jurisdictions with both daily HR matters and large-scale organisational transformations.

Employers today face challenges such as rapidly evolving labour laws, growing union influence, complex workforce mobility, and the need to retain top talent in competitive global markets. Our services help companies navigate these challenges with confidence and efficiency.

We support multinational businesses in collective bargaining, works council negotiations, compliance with labour standards, and restructuring processes. Our team also advises companies on mobility strategies, helping them deploy talent in the right markets while maintaining full regulatory compliance.

Workforce Strategy & HR Advisory

We assist with day-to-day human resources matters, drafting employment contracts, internal regulations, and workplace policies. Our team helps employers design reward strategies, performance frameworks, and retention mechanisms.

Labour Disputes & Industrial Relations

We represent employers in labour disputes, collective negotiations, and conflict prevention strategies. We help companies build efficient and sustainable labour relations while protecting their operational interests.

Global Mobility & Multijurisdictional Assignments

We support companies relocating employees, managing expatriate assignments, and complying with immigration and labour requirements across borders. Our experience spans several industries and jurisdictions, allowing us to provide tailored mobility solutions.
