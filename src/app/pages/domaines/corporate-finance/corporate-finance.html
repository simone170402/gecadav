Corporate Finance

Introduction

At Tsapy & Partners, we offer expert legal support in corporate finance matters, including fundraising, grants, and financial structuring. Whether your organisation is expanding, restructuring, or seeking capital, we help secure the financial backing you need with clarity and confidence.

Many financing agreements involve complex contractual requirements. Banks and donors often impose standard documentation that may not always align with the client’s operational needs. Our lawyers analyse these contracts, highlight unnecessary or excessive clauses, and negotiate more suitable terms where possible.

Our Corporate Finance Services Include

  • Assistance with grant applications
  • Review and negotiation of financing agreements
  • Risk analysis of bank documentation
  • Structuring of fundraising operations
  • Advice on compliance and reporting obligations
