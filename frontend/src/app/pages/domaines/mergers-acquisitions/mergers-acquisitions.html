Mergers & Acquisitions

Introduction

Mergers and acquisition transactions are highly strategic operations that require precision, experience, and efficiency to maximize deal value. At Tsapy & Partners, we rely on our strong cross-border capabilities and deep sector knowledge to guide clients through every stage of their transactions, ensuring optimal outcomes and sustainable synergies.

Our M&A team provides seamless advice by combining local expertise with international standards. We collaborate closely with specialists in tax, competition, employment, intellectual property, foreign investment, and commercial law to address all legal, regulatory, and commercial implications of each transaction.

As one of the few firms with a dedicated global reorganizations practice, we support clients throughout the full transaction lifecycle—from deal shaping and asset carving, to negotiation, closing, and post-transaction integration.

Cross-Border & Strategic Transactions

Our lawyers have extensive experience handling complex cross-border mergers, acquisitions, divestitures, restructurings, and joint venture operations. We anticipate potential deal risks, ensure regulatory compliance, and secure maximum transaction value.

Regulatory, Tax & Compliance Insights

M&A deals trigger numerous legal and regulatory consequences. Our interdisciplinary team works together to analyse tax exposures, competition law implications, labour responsibilities, intellectual property transfers, and foreign investment regulations.

This integrated approach ensures that our clients receive strategic, practical, and fully compliant advice.

Post-Transaction Support

We assist with post-merger integration, restructuring, asset consolidation, governance reorganization, and the alignment of operational structures with newly acquired entities. Our objective is to secure the long-term value and synergies of each transaction.
