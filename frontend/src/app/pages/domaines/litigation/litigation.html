Criminal Litigation

Criminal litigation is often highly sensitive, particularly because individuals may face prosecution while free or in detention throughout the proceedings. Many defendants unknowingly make irreversible mistakes during the investigation phase due to lack of early legal assistance.

Our specialized advocates support clients from the earliest stages of criminal prosecution, ensuring that due process is respected and that their fundamental rights are not violated. We accompany clients throughout the investigation, trial, and appeal processes.

Civil Litigation

Civil litigation encompasses disputes where parties primarily seek compensation or damages. This includes divorce, alimony, succession disputes, paternity matters, reconstruction of civil status documents, and breaches of contractual obligations.

In Cameroon, land-related disputes represent the majority of civil litigation. Due to administrative deficiencies in land registration, multiple buyers may unknowingly acquire land already titled to another party, or purchase land that is subject to succession disputes.

Our firm assists clients in land acquisition, land registration, and land-related litigation to ensure secure and compliant land ownership.

Administrative Litigation

Administrative courts in Cameroon handle disputes arising from municipal and regional elections, as well as actions involving the State, decentralized public entities, and public institutions. These courts frequently intervene in cases seeking the annulment of ministerial decrees granting land titles obtained fraudulently or through misrepresentation.

We represent clients whose interests are in conflict with those of the State in administrative proceedings, ensuring that administrative actions comply with the law and respect the rights of individuals.
