Intellectual Property

Introduction

Intellectual property is one of the most dynamic and disruptive areas of modern law. Our firm provides agile and forward-thinking IP protection to ensure that our clients’ creative and commercial assets remain secure in an increasingly competitive global environment.

We represent companies in IP litigation and advisory matters, offering end-to-end support from prosecution to enforcement, strategy, and defense against infringement claims.

Trademark & Copyright Litigation

Our experienced IP litigators defend and enforce the rights of clients across Africa. We handle trademark infringements, copyright disputes, unfair competition matters, and complex litigation involving digital and creative assets.

With deep knowledge of the evolving IP landscape, we help clients mitigate risks, preserve market value, and respond effectively to violations.

IP Strategy, Counseling & Prosecution

In today's fast-moving global marketplace, IP issues can arise in any jurisdiction. We assist clients in building strong IP portfolios through trademark registration, copyright protection, design filings, and continuous monitoring.
