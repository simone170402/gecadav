Human Rights Consultancy & Contentious Work

Introduction

Advocating for human rights requires rigorous research, technical expertise, and deep legal insight. Contrary to popular belief, human rights work involves much more than advocacy campaigns — it includes legal analysis, complex reporting, litigation, data collection, and continuous field engagement.

Since hosting the first office of the Human Rights League in 1993, Tsapy & Partners has supported numerous NGOs, international organisations, and civil society actors in the promotion and protection of human rights across Africa.

Our services span gender equality, child protection, domestic violence, internally displaced persons (IDPs), fair trial rights, and access to justice. We provide high-quality research and technical assistance tailored to each organisation’s needs.

Our Services Include

  • Legal and statistical research
  • Drafting of reports, studies and policy documents
  • Gender mainstreaming and gender-sensitive programming
  • Child protection and child labour prevention
  • Domestic violence prevention frameworks
  • Access to justice and fair trial evaluations
  • Human rights litigation and legal representation
