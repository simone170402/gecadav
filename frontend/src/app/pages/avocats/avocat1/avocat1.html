Founder & Head of Cabinet Tsapy & Partners

Joseph Lavoisier Tsapy is the Founder and Head of Cabinet Tsapy & Partners, and a senior member of the Cameroon Bar Association. He holds a Master’s Degree in Criminal Sciences from the University of Yaoundé, as well as a Diploma in Specialized Criminal Sciences obtained from the University of Dakar, Senegal.

Called to the Bar in 1991, Me Tsapy has over three decades of distinguished legal practice, during which he has built a solid reputation for rigor, integrity, and unwavering commitment to justice. His professional career spans a wide range of legal disciplines, with particular expertise in criminal law, civil law, public international law, and human rights.

Beyond his courtroom practice, Me Tsapy has played a significant role in the institutional development of the legal profession in Cameroon. He is the Founder and first President of the Ligue des Droits de l’Homme in Cameroon, established in 1993, reflecting his long-standing dedication to the promotion and protection of fundamental rights.

He has also served in several leadership and advisory capacities within the legal community, including as Former President of the Commission for the Training of Lawyers, and President of the Commission for the Resolution of Disputes between Lawyers in the West Region of Cameroon. Through these roles, he has contributed actively to professional ethics, legal education, and the strengthening of the rule of law.

Me Tsapy has benefited from international exposure through professional training programs, including internships and capacity-building initiatives in the United States in the field of civil society organization management, as well as specialized programs on decentralization in Germany, in collaboration with international foundations.

In recognition of his professional excellence and societal contribution, he has received several distinctions, including an award recognizing him as Best Lawyer of the West Region of Cameroon, as well as official acknowledgment for his contribution to national reflections on the human rights situation in Cameroon.

Today, Me Joseph Lavoisier Tsapy is widely regarded as a leading authority in criminal justice and human rights, combining deep legal expertise with strategic insight and a strong commitment to ethical advocacy.
