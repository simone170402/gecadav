Managing Partner – Cabinet Tsapy

Tsapy Djihafia Lavoisier Junior is the Managing Partner of Cabinet Tsapy in Cameroon. He is a member of both the Cameroon Bar Association and the Rwanda Bar Association, as well as a member of the East African Law Society, reflecting his strong regional and cross-border legal practice.

He holds a Bachelor’s Degree in English Law from the University of Yaoundé II, Soa, and a Postgraduate Diploma in Legal Practice from the Institute of Legal Practice and Development (ILPD) in Rwanda. His academic background combines common law training with practical legal education in a regional context.

Me Tsapy Lavoisier Junior has developed professional experience in several areas of practice, with a particular focus on family law, mediation, and dispute resolution. He is known for a pragmatic, solution-oriented approach, emphasizing negotiation and amicable settlement where appropriate.

In addition to his legal practice, he has demonstrated strong leadership and public engagement. He is a Former President of the Commonwealth Students Union and a former member of the National Youth Council, experiences that have strengthened his skills in advocacy, governance, and institutional dialogue.

As Managing Partner, Me Tsapy Djihafia Lavoisier Junior plays a central role in the strategic development of Cabinet Tsapy, overseeing client relations, cross-border matters, and the coordination of the firm’s legal teams, while upholding the firm’s core values of professionalism, integrity, and excellence.
